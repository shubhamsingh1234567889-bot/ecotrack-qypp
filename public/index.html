      Carbon credits, explained

      Learn before you buy

      A carbon credit generally represents one metric tonne of CO₂ equivalent reduced or removed under a defined program. Credits are not the same as reducing your own emissions, and quality varies.

      01Measure first

      Estimate your footprint and reduce avoidable emissions before considering credits.

      02Check the standard

      Look for independent verification, clear project details, additionality, permanence and safeguards.

      03Check the registry

      Confirm serial numbers, ownership and retirement status. Avoid double counting and guaranteed-return claims.

      EcoTrack does not issue credits or certify projects. A personal footprint estimate alone is not enough to calculate credits eligible for sale. Businesses should consult a qualified carbon-accounting professional for formal claims.
